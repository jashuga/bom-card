## Inspiration
We were inspired by classic arcade shooters and newer deckbuilding and roguelike experiences to combine fast action with deliberate strategy.

## What it does
Our project is a playable game, a fusion of classic arcade shooters and deckbuilding genres

## How we built it
We built the game entirely in Godot, using C#.

## Challenges we ran into
Most of the challenges we ran into were of scope and direction. For example, deciding whether the player could only shoot along one axis, and focusing on polishing the existing mechanics instead of adding more.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of
Some of the deeper implementation details of the game's inner workings were especially fun and challenging to figure out. The deckbuilding portion meshes with the enemy waves, involving the lists of ammo and enemy types all in one system

## What we learned
This was many of our teams' first experience with Godot, and together we quickly learned to put together scenes and nodes into a complete game.
